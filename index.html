05 · Solana Foundation · 2025

Solana Gym

An RL gym for LLMs on Solana. Agents write TypeScript, it runs on a Surfpool mainnet fork, and they score unique instructions that actually land. Two tracks: Basic and Swap. Curiosity reward, not PnL.

Basic median scores: claude-sonnet-4 115, gpt-5 60, gemini-2.5-flash 40.
